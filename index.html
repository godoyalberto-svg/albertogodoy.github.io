About Me

Raised on mountains,
guided by light

I grew up in Montana, surrounded by some of the most breathtaking scenery in the country. My grandfather and my dad were always behind a camera or a camcorder — documenting life, chasing landscapes, preserving moments. That instinct was passed down to me before I ever knew what to call it.

When my dad was at work, I'd quietly pick up his camera and head outside. The mountains were my first subject and my first teacher. I learned to read the sky, to wait for the right moment, to let the light do the heavy lifting. That philosophy never left me — whether I'm shooting a first dance, a concert stage, or a portrait in an open field, I still let light lead the way.

Now based in Atlanta, I shoot professionally and pour everything into building something of my own — a business that carries the same honesty and care my family put into every photo they ever took. I shoot on Nikon Z-series mirrorless and edit with a natural, true-to-life touch — no heavy filters, no manufactured moments. Just you, the light, and the real thing.
